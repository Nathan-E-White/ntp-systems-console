01 / Purpose

Purpose and Decision Context

The workbench is designed to support a short engine-systems review narrative: define an operating case, assemble synthetic evidence from three analysis families, identify the controlling concern, and recommend the next analysis needed to retire risk. It demonstrates systems-analysis judgment and traceability rather than production solver execution.

Customer question

Can the engine establish and maintain the requested startup or restart posture without consuming thermal, hydraulic, or stability margin?

Review product

A concise statement of operating posture, supporting evidence, assumptions, controlling condition, and discipline-specific follow-up actions.

Claim boundary

No criticality, safety, shielding, materials qualification, computational validation, or flight-design conclusion is supported.

Companion documentsThe broader governing physics and fixture interpretation are maintained in Physics Behind the NTP Analysis Fixtures. The exact equations executed by the dashboard, including empirical coefficients and worked substitutions, are maintained in Reduced-Order Engine Model Basis.

05 / Transport Model

MCNP-Like Geometry and Evidence Definition

The MCNP-like fixture family spatializes the engine network into material regions and tally ownership. Its active core is reduced to three axial zones and six azimuthal sector proxies per zone. Two companion workflows now share that geometry: ntp_mcnp.inp prescribes a fixed neutron source, while ntp_crit.inp introduces parser-facing KCODE, KSRC, burnable-cell, and depletion-study records.

Fixed-source fixture

ntp_mcnp

MODE N, SDEF, cell-average flux proxies, and component-level transport handoffs. It does not estimate multiplication.

Criticality fixture

ntp_crit

KCODE 5000 1.0 50 250, five axial KSRC points, 18 burnable core cells, and a six-step synthetic depletion grid.

Claim boundary

Parser evidence only

The reported k_eff, cycle statistics, drum reserve, xenon worth, inventories, and decay heat are fabricated workflow records, not MCNP results.

06 / Field Model

MOOSE-Like Thermal and Multiphysics Definition

The MOOSE-like fixture represents the receiving field model. Primary variables are temperatures; auxiliary variables carry system schedules and review proxies. Its kernel pattern describes transient storage, conduction, and prescribed sources.

Visible fixture inconsistencyThe current MOOSE-like input is not necessarily executable. Structural margins are especially unsupported because the file defines no displacement variables, mechanics kernels, constitutive response, contact, or qualified failure criteria.

See physics: thermal conduction and channel heat transfer, physics: thermomechanics, and physics: numerical methods.

08 / Historical Data Lineage

Non-Exhaustive Sources of Real Validation Evidence

The synthetic fixtures deliberately stop short of design use. Before any real design claim, an engineering team would need to replace the fabricated tables with controlled evidence from historical test programs, code benchmarks, fuel and material campaigns, facility data, and independently reviewed modern analyses. The entries below are not treated as equivalent data sources; they are a medium-depth map of where a reviewer might begin looking for real evidence, limitations, and historical precedent.

09 / Integrated Review

Review Logic and Recommended Use

1. Objective

State the requested operating condition and customer decision.

2. Posture

Summarize power, flow, thrust, temperature, pressure drop, and stability signals.

3. Evidence

Identify which model or fixture supplies each supporting result.

4. Concern

Name the controlling mechanism, not merely the lowest dashboard score.

5. Limits

Expose prescribed states, model-form assumptions, and missing qualification.

6. Trades

Recommend flow, power, geometry, controls, materials, or cycle sensitivities.

7. Evidence plan

Specify the next analysis, benchmark, or test needed to retire uncertainty.

8. Decision

Proceed, hold, or revise the case with explicit rationale and ownership.

Preferred demo conclusionThe investigation case is valuable because it demonstrates how adverse thermal and hydraulic signals are traced across disciplines and converted into a focused analysis plan. The fixture does not predict an engine instability or establish a design limit.
